## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are field workers on construction sites, usually on phones, often outdoors, moving quickly, and needing to clock in, capture proof of work, check tasks, and confirm hours without friction. Managers use a separate dashboard, but the worker flow should feel continuous from sign-in through checkout.

### Brand Personality
Fast, disciplined, and job-site tough. The product should feel trustworthy, clear under pressure, and slightly industrial rather than playful. It should project urgency and confidence without looking flashy or consumer-social.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark-first, finance-inspired operational UI adapted for construction work: dense but readable, high contrast, restrained surfaces, and a single yellow accent for action. The interface should feel like a crew tool used before sunrise and at the tail end of a shift, not a marketing site.

### Design Principles
1. Keep the worker flow continuous: auth, clock, journal, tasks, and hours should read as one system.
2. Make time state obvious at a glance: current shift, project, elapsed time, and next required action should always be easy to find.
3. Favor mobile clarity over dashboard complexity: large touch targets, compact copy, and stable layouts.
4. Use the ledger as the source of truth: hours, checkout state, and history should derive from real events.
5. Stay visually restrained: dark surfaces, whisper-light depth, and Binance yellow as the only true accent.
